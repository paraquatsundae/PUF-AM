MODULE: PUFAM (Ag Manager)
ROLE: Multi-enterprise farm / paddock management web app
STATUS: app (live on Cloud Run; Capacitor Android)

ONE-LINER
Paddock-first Ag Manager for mixed farms — map areas and issues, farm diary, seasonal records, and optional crop packs (walnut blight first).

SYNOPSIS
- Ag Manager — multi-user farm ops (public name PUFAM; repo PUF-AM). Formerly PUFOM / Orchard Manager.
- Brand: PUFAM = PUF (Prototype Until Failure workshop) + AM (Ag Manager); in-app PUF emu mark, green farm icon on Android.
- Mixed enterprises: orchard/tree, fruit, vineyard, broadacre, hort, station/grazing, aquaculture (marron), livestock overlay.
- Farm map: draw areas, pin issues, infrastructure (dams, irrigation, tracks); optional offline basemap packs. Diary is the system of record for sprays, irrigation, nutrition, and work.
- Crop packs are optional — farm admins install them under Settings → Plugins. Walnut blight (Ji et al. 2025 on proxy wetness, not LLM) is the first pack and only shows when that pack is on.
- Also: seasonal water budget, harvest/drying (dryer moisture model), invite-PIN team auth (admin / farmer / viewer).
- Workshop path: local-first records plus optional Freenet/mist so sealed copies can live across farms and devices — share the load, less locked to a rented data centre. Cloud PIN login at am.pufworks.farm remains the simple path today.
- Web at am.pufworks.farm; Android APK via GitHub Releases. Standalone Dynamic chill calculator at pufworks.farm/chill/.
- Does NOT control sprayers, ISOBUS, or vision — companion farm ops product under the same PUF workshop brand.
