MODULE: PUFOM (Walnut Farm Manager)
ROLE: Orchard / paddock management web app
STATUS: app (live on Cloud Run; Capacitor Android)

ONE-LINER
Multi-user orchard management for walnut farms — map blocks and issues, farm diary, and blight management from weather and spray records.

SYNOPSIS
- Orchard Manager — multi-user farm management system focused on walnut orchard management (public name PUFOM; repo Walnut_farm_manager).
- Blight management tool: weather-driven infection-risk modelling tied to the farm diary and spray records; Ji et al. 2025 mechanistic model on a proxy wetness input — not LLM/AI predictions.
- Orchard map: draw blocks, pin field issues, optional offline basemap packs; issues feed into diary plans (Capacitor mobile path supported).
- Farm diary is system of record for sprays, irrigation, nutrition, and work.
- Also covers harvest/drying (dryer performance model), water budget from farm setup allocation, nutrition logging, financials, invite-PIN team auth.
- Stack: React/Vite/TypeScript, Firebase Firestore + Auth, DPIRD weather via server cache/Cloud Functions; deployed to Google Cloud Run (australia-southeast1).
- Does NOT control sprayers, ISOBUS, or vision — separate from the PUFworks cab stack (companion orchard ops product under same PUF workshop brand).
